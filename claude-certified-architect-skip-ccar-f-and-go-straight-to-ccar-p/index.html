Claude Certified Architect: Skip CCAR-F and Go Straight to CCAR-P

AI Mon 05 October 2026 · 3 min read · Ashley Kleynhans

I was recently on the bench between projects at work. My employer encourages consultants to use bench time for certifications and will reimburse the cost, so I decided to go after the Claude Certified Architect certification from Anthropic. In this post I want to share my recommendation on which exam to sit, how I prepared, and what to expect.

Skip CCAR-F, Go Straight to CCAR-P

Anthropic offers two levels of this certification: CCAR-F, the foundations level, and CCAR-P, the professional level. My recommendation for anyone with senior engineering experience is to skip CCAR-F entirely and go straight to CCAR-P.

A lot of the CCAR-P material overlaps with CCAR-F, and most of the additional professional-level content covers things that experienced engineers already run into in their day to day work, such as system design trade-offs, production considerations, and architectural decision-making. In my own experience I found CCAR-P to actually be easier than CCAR-F, and I scored higher on it. If you already have solid engineering experience, the foundations exam is unlikely to teach you much that the professional exam does not already cover at a more relevant level.

How I Prepared

I spent about 24 to 30 hours preparing for the CCAR-P exam, using the following resources:

  1. The Anthropic recommended material on Skilljar: Claude Certified Architect Professional learning path
  2. Jacob Bushong's Udemy course: CCAR-P Exam Prep
  3. Jacob Bushong's set of practice tests on Udemy: CCAR-P 6 Practice Exams
  4. Frank Kane's set of practice tests on Udemy: Claude Certified Architect Professional Practice Exam Pack

Working through the official Skilljar path first gave me the grounding in Anthropic's own material, and the Udemy course filled in the detail with a more structured, exam-focused walkthrough. The two sets of practice tests were useful for different reasons: Jacob Bushong's exams tracked closely to the structure of his course, while Frank Kane's pack gave me a slightly different angle on the same topics, which helped surface gaps that a single question bank would not have caught.

A Cheaper Way to Access the Udemy Courses

Rather than buying each Udemy course individually, I purchased a Udemy subscription for R329 a month, which worked out cheaper overall since it gave me access to all of the above courses and practice tests.

Practice Exams Are Not a Perfect Reflection of the Real Thing

It is worth noting that none of the practice exams I used, from either Jacob Bushong or Frank Kane, are a 100% reflection of the real CCAR-P exam. In my experience the real exam is a bit more difficult than any of the practice material. Treat the practice tests as a way to reinforce your knowledge and find gaps, not as a reliable predictor of your final score.

Summary

If you are a senior engineer considering this certification path:

  1. Skip CCAR-F and go straight to CCAR-P. The overlap is substantial and the additional professional-level content plays to the strengths you already have.
  2. Start with the official Anthropic material on Skilljar before moving on to third-party courses.
  3. Use more than one set of practice tests to get broader coverage of question styles.
  4. If you plan to take more than one Udemy course, compare the cost of a monthly subscription against buying courses individually.
  5. Expect the real exam to be somewhat harder than the practice tests, and prepare accordingly.

Good luck if you are preparing for CCAR-P.
